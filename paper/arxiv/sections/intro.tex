\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:intro}

Autonomous LLM agents are increasingly deployed to act on behalf of a principal---a
firm, a user, an operator---in settings where they encounter \emph{other} agents pursuing
\emph{other} principals' goals. A growing literature establishes that such agents will
cooperate in social dilemmas \citep{akata2025repeated,madmoun2025communication} and will
collude on price without being told to \citep{fish2024algorithmic,calvano2020ai}---echoing
classical results that repeated interaction sustains cooperation
\citep{axelrod1984evolution} and that even costless ``cheap-talk'' communication has
strategic, sometimes adversarial, value \citep{schelling1960strategy,crawford1982strategic}.
The
open and safety-relevant question is no longer \emph{whether} this happens, but \emph{what
makes it happen}: which features of the interface we build around these agents tip
ordinary strategic interaction into coordination that harms the very principal the agent
is supposed to serve.

We argue that two communication \emph{affordances} are the decisive levers, and that they
can be varied independently:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.1em,itemsep=1pt,topsep=2pt]
\item \textbf{Semantic bandwidth} --- how much \emph{content} an agent can convey:
nothing, a fixed menu signal, constrained free text, or open free text.
\item \textbf{Observation / accountability} --- whether the principal (or an overseer)
can read and audit what the agents say and do.
\end{itemize}
\noindent Our central thesis is that of these two axes, \emph{semantic bandwidth---content---is
the dominant lever}, while observation/accountability is a \emph{weaker and unreliable}
control. Content converts strategic interaction into \emph{principal-harming coordination}
across three distinct surfaces---cooperation in social dilemmas, supracompetitive price
collusion, and instruction-literal misalignment. Observation, by contrast, repeatedly records
harm without deterring it: passive monitoring detects collusion but does not stop it
(enforcement can deter, though the effect is cell-dependent), and making an agent's reporting
auditable does not stop it from passing along a misleading status delivered as \emph{ambient
context}. A firm operator instruction is a strong but \emph{literal} guardrail---it resists a
direct request to do wrong, but harm slips through the indirect/ambient routes it never
named---a behavior anticipated by work on instruction-literal reward hacking
\citep{hubinger2019risks} and goal misgeneralization
\citep{langosco2022goal,shah2022goal}.

\paragraph{This paper began as a different question.}
We set out to ask ``when do rival AIs cooperate, and does national origin of the model
matter?'' Mid-project we corrected the question to ``does communication pull agents to
act against their operator?''; following external review we adopt the sharper
affordance/accountability framing above. We are explicit about this history because it
shapes what we treat as confirmatory versus exploratory (\S\ref{sec:apparatus}), and
because it explains why a model-\emph{origin} hypothesis that motivated the original design
is here \emph{demoted} to a bounded within-sample case study (\S\ref{sec:origin}).

\paragraph{Contributions.}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.3em,itemsep=2pt,topsep=2pt]
\item \textbf{An affordance ladder: free text $\gg$ menu signal}~(\tC). We isolate
bandwidth from observability and from authorship. A fixed-menu intention signal \emph{does
not} improve cooperation (it trends below the no-channel baseline, but the per-cell contrast
is not significant); the robust, fully significant effect is that free text far exceeds the
menu signal (L2 vs.\ L1 risk difference $+0.60$ $[0.33,0.78]$, $p{=}3{\times}10^{-5}$). We
lead with that contrast. Consistent with this, the lever is open \emph{content}---menu and
self-authored action-only messages both behave like silence---and the effect survives on
novel payoffs and across lock-in thresholds (\S\ref{sec:results}).
\item \textbf{Channel-driven price collusion that passive oversight does not
deter}~(\tC). Across four market mechanisms a channel lifts collusion from price-war to
supracompetitive, including a sustained \emph{supra-monopoly} ($K{>}1$) regime we report
honestly rather than clip. A read-only monitor detects but does not deter; only enforcement
does. Against a clean control the monitor's score is \emph{discriminative} (AUC $0.88$) but
\emph{mis-thresholded}---correcting our own initial ``flag-everything'' reading---and
calibrating the threshold drives false positives to zero (\S\ref{sec:oversight}).
\item \textbf{Instruction literalism, and an ambient-context manipulation route}~(\tE). We
map a dose-response curve for a confidential-information leak and identify the precise
boundary of a firm operator instruction: it resists empathy, reciprocity, and direct
pleading---and agents refuse a \emph{direct} request to lie---but the same agents passively
launder a misleading status delivered as \emph{ambient context}. We report a
delivery-controlled de-confound ($n{=}30$, powered to $48$) that overturns our own initial
``observability gates honesty'' reading: making the channel auditable does not fix the ambient
route, so the mechanism is a \emph{refusal-affordance / request-modality} effect. We scope this
as an \emph{exploratory boundary condition}---prompt-specific, observed in a single sandboxed
task family (\S\ref{sec:alignment}).
\item \textbf{Frontier-model generality, and independent supracompetitive pricing}~(\tE).
Re-running the spine on GPT-4o, Claude-3.7, and a Claude$\times$Llama cross-pair, the
principal-harming \emph{collusion} effect generalizes ($0.19\to0.81$ on GPT-4o). A separate,
striking result: some frontier models (Claude) price supracompetitively ($K{=}1.29$)
\emph{with no communication channel at all}---but a coordination diagnostic shows this is
\emph{independent} high pricing (both agents play the grid-max price from round 1, zero
convergence), a fixed disposition rather than tacit coordination. Genuine
convergence/coordination appears only on the open and GPT-4o spine \emph{with} a channel. The
\emph{cooperation} effect, by contrast, is open-model-specific (GPT-4o cooperates
unconditionally; Claude$\times$Llama never does). A powered elicitation (C8) further shows
covert steganographic collusion is a \emph{latent capability}---absent spontaneously, present
on demand (\S\ref{sec:generality}).
\item \textbf{A reproducible apparatus and disclosure standard.} A pre-registered
confirmatory spine, Wilson intervals on bimodal outcomes, direct contrast tests
(Fisher exact, risk differences, bootstrap CIs on $K$), an honestly-reported under-powered
equivalence (TOST) attempt for the origin null, refusal coding, novel-demand anti-memorization
controls, and a full statistics appendix (\S\ref{sec:repro}, App.~\ref{app:stats}), with
prompts, seeds, and raw tables released.
\end{enumerate}

\paragraph{The throughline, sharpened.}
The spine of this work is one question: \emph{can rival AIs cooperate or collude in surprising,
principal-harming ways?} A second wave of experiments sharpened the answer rather than blunting
it. The most capable models we tested reach supracompetitive prices with no channel at all---an
\emph{independent} high-pricing disposition rather than coordination (the harmful outcome is real
either way); they can \emph{hide} channel-driven collusion on demand (a latent covert
capability); and naive oversight \emph{detects but does not reliably stop} them---the monitor is
discriminative yet mis-thresholded, and enforcement deters only in some cells. The honesty
failure (passing along a misleading status delivered as an ambient artifact) and the latent
covert capability are deployment-relevant edges of that one question. None of these are
reassuring; all are scoped, tiered, and released as exploratory boundary conditions.

\paragraph{Tiering.}
We separate two evidential speeds and label every quantitative claim. \textbf{Confirmatory}
results~(\tC) come from a pre-registered spine (experiments A1, B1, A3) run on fresh
held-out seeds (offset 100), with thresholds and analyses fixed in advance.
\textbf{Exploratory} results~(\tE) come from the wider catalog ($\sim$30 experiment
families) and are reported as hypotheses, never with confirmatory stats. All claims are
scoped to ``these four models, in English, with this scaffold.''
