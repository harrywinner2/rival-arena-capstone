\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We reframed ``when do rival AIs cooperate?'' into a sharper, safety-relevant question: which
interface affordances convert strategic interaction into coordination that harms the
principal? Two answers stand on a pre-registered confirmatory spine. First, open
\emph{content} is the dominant variable for cooperation and collusion: a free-text channel
raises cooperation and rival pricing toward supracompetitive far above a fixed menu signal
(L2 $\gg$ L1, $p{=}3{\times}10^{-5}$), the menu does not improve on silence, and the effects
survive payoffs and demand the models cannot have memorized and every lock-in threshold.
Second, passive oversight is inadequate as deployed: a read-only monitor detects collusion at
near-ceiling rates but deters it by approximately zero; enforcement can deter, though
cell-dependently. The monitor's score is in fact discriminative (AUC $0.88$ against a designed
control) but mis-thresholded---correcting our own earlier ``flag-everything'' reading---so
calibrating the threshold drives false positives to zero. Third, exploratorily, a firm operator
instruction is a strong but \emph{literal} guardrail---it resists empathy, reciprocity,
pleading, and even a \emph{direct} request to lie, yet the same agents passively pass along a
misleading status delivered as \emph{ambient context}. A delivery-controlled de-confound
($n{=}30$, powered to $48$) overturned our own initial ``observability gates honesty''
reading---auditing the channel does not fix the ambient route---leaving a refusal-affordance /
request-modality mechanism, which we scope as an exploratory boundary condition in a single
task family. The practical takeaways are concrete: constrained ``coordination signals'' should
not be expected to help, surveillance without enforcement does not deter, and guardrails
protect only the routes they \emph{name}---observation, across collusion and honesty alike, is
a weak and unreliable control. We release the
apparatus, prompts, seeds, and raw tables so these claims---each scoped to these models and
this arena---can be checked, broken, and extended.
