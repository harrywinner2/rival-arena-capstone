% Abstract — confirmatory-first (question -> confirmatory findings -> one line of exploratory).
We ask not \emph{whether} rival LLM agents cooperate but \emph{which interface
affordances} convert their strategic interaction into coordination that harms the
principal who deploys them, varying two affordances independently---\emph{semantic
bandwidth} (no channel $\to$ menu signal $\to$ free text) and \emph{observation /
accountability} (whether the principal can read and audit the channel)---in a factorial
``rival arena'' with a pre-registered confirmatory spine on fresh held-out seeds. We
report match-level lock-in proportions with Wilson intervals (matches are bimodal) and
direct contrast tests. Three confirmatory findings stand.~\tC{} \textbf{(1)} A free-text
channel raises cooperation in the iterated Prisoner's Dilemma (lock-in $0.15\to0.60$;
Fisher $p{=}0.008$) and raises pricing toward supracompetitive in a Bertrand duopoly
(collusion index $K$ rises with the channel, $L3{-}L0$ on novel demand $+0.27$,
$p{=}2{\times}10^{-4}$; up to a supra-monopoly $K{=}1.23$ $[0.95,1.54]$ on canonical
demand). A fixed-menu signal, by contrast, \emph{does not} improve cooperation and trends
below silence (L1 $0/20$ vs.\ L0 $3/20$, n.s.); the robust, fully significant contrast is
that \emph{free text} $\gg$ \emph{menu signal} (L2 vs.\ L1, $+0.60$ $[0.33,0.78]$,
$p{=}3{\times}10^{-5}$). \textbf{(2)} A read-only monitor detects collusion at
near-ceiling rates but does not deter it; enforcement can deter (a penalty cuts $K$
$0.33\to0.18$) though deterrence is cell-dependent. \textbf{(3)} Origin moderation of the
channel effect is non-significant and under-powered. \emph{Exploratorily}~\tE, the
collusion effect replicates on GPT-4o ($0.19\to0.81$), some frontier models (Claude) price
supracompetitively without any channel via a fixed disposition rather than coordination,
covert collusion is a latent capability present only when elicited, and agents that refuse
a direct request to lie still pass along a misleading status delivered as \emph{ambient
context}---a boundary condition observed in a single sandboxed task family. We release
prompts, model IDs, seeds, manifests, raw tables, and figure scripts; every number is
tier-labeled confirmatory~\tC{} or exploratory~\tE{}, scoped to these models and this arena.
