# Rule-timing diagnostics

Free-text matches only (L2/L3). Explains the reversed mediation sign.


## H1 — Are rules a *response* to defection?

- IPD free-text matches analysed: 540
- matches containing >=1 rule: 93 (17.2%)
- of those with a defection, rule appears **after** the first defection in 69.3% of matches, before it in 30.7%

**Round-level hazard.** P(rule this round | a defection already happened) = 0.020 (n=4424) vs P(rule | no defection yet) = 0.018 (n=3672); difference **+0.002** [-0.004, +0.008].


## H2 — Do rules help *after* a deviation? (recovery analysis)

For each round where a defection has already occurred, does a contingent-punishment rule uttered at round t predict mutual cooperation at t+1?

- post-deviation rounds analysed: 4038
- P(mutual cooperation next round | rule sent) = 0.293 (n=82)
- P(mutual cooperation next round | no rule) = 0.399 (n=3956)
- **difference -0.106, 95% CI [-0.204, -0.004]**

Restricted to rounds where a defection occurred *this* round (n=2516): rule 0.164 vs no-rule 0.116, difference **+0.048** [-0.036, +0.142]


## Alternative mediator — explicit joint-plan / agreement proposals

- IPD free-text, agreement present n=474, lock-in 0.591; absent n=66, lock-in 0.561
- **difference +0.030, 95% CI [-0.099, +0.156]**
